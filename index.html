Question & answer (Q&A)

Common questions
What is this, in plain terms? ▾
A structured AI development team you can stand up when you do not have engineers on payroll. Roles mirror real dev org functions (research, planning, implementation, QA, docs, etc.) so you get multi-step software work without pretending one generic chat is every discipline at once.
How does the team communicate with me, the user? ▾
Through a user channel: plain-language updates, questions when requirements are unclear, options when there are tradeoffs, and approvals before risky changes. You stay the product owner; the team surfaces what it needs (access, priorities, constraints) instead of guessing in silence.
How do agents communicate with each other? ▾
Through internal handoffs: structured messages or tickets (briefs, plans, diffs, test reports), plus a shared Memory layer so context survives across sessions. Research passes artifacts to Planner; Execution ships to Tester and Critic; Critic sends findings back to Execution or to you when a decision is required.
How do they work together on a common goal? ▾
They run a team loop with shared artifacts. You give the goal and constraints. Research turns them into a structured brief and flags unknowns. Planner converts the brief into a plan with milestones, risks, and acceptance criteria. Execution implements the work and produces concrete outputs (diffs, scripts, and handoff notes). Tester validates the outputs against the acceptance criteria. Critic performs a review pass for correctness, edge cases, and security. Memory stores what happened so the next iteration is faster and consistent. Finally, Writer/Coder packages what you need to use and what to do next.
Why is each role expected to be deeply knowledgeable? ▾
So quality stays high without a human manager in the loop. Each agent is chartered as a subject-matter lead for its title: correct terminology, best practices, edge cases, and clear quality bars—whether the work is code, tests, or documentation.
Can the team work offline? ▾
Yes, when you implement it that way: local models, bundled references, cached docs, and on-disk memory. Online mode adds live search and browser-style verification. This HTML page itself works fully offline once saved on your machine.
Why split Research, Planner, Execution, and Critic? ▾
Same reason real teams separate those concerns: fewer missed requirements, clearer plans, safer delivery, and a dedicated review pass before you ship—especially important when you are the only human in the room.
Is this tied to another product or blockchain project? ▾
No. This UI describes a standalone AI dev-team concept. You can copy this folder into its own repository and evolve it without coupling to any unrelated codebase.
