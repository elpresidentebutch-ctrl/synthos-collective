Administrator service validator

Push the button. Run a real background node.

The button downloads a Windows installer for a local validator service. The service creates its own Ed25519 key and sends real signed heartbeats every 15 seconds while the computer is on — no website login and no browser tab required.

Validator reward rule

Approved validator operators may earn 5,000 SYN/month base, with up to 7,500 SYN/month maximum after one full month of verified uptime.

No payment is made for clicking the button alone. Rewards require the installed runner to keep sending real signed uptime proofs.
