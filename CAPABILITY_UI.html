v4: Orchestration Layer

Capability Registry

Canonical registry of autonomous actions. Agents discover these capabilities to compose complex intents.

stake(amount, peer)
Binds SYN liquidity to a peer node to increase network validation weight and earn rewards.
vote(proposal, side)
Casts a cryptographic ballot on active governance proposals. Weight is derived from stake.
rebalance(target)
High-level capability to shift liquidity across anchors for optimal liveness resilience.
detect_anomaly(peer)
Uses AI inference to monitor peer gossip for behavioral deviations or liveness risk.
