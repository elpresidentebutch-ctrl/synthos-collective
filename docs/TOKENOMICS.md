## 7. Immune Node Utility

The immune node is the core utility mechanism for SYNTHOS.

An immune node should make SYNTHOS useful by performing verifiable network-support work. That work may include:

- creating local hardware or device commitments;
- submitting heartbeat proofs;
- supporting validator, adopter, or network-health checks;
- helping identify unhealthy, unavailable, or suspicious network behavior;
- strengthening decentralization by allowing more people to participate in network operations; and
- supporting the long-term security and reliability of the SYNTHOS ecosystem.

The intended economic loop is:

1. A participant runs an immune node.
2. The immune node performs verifiable network-support work.
3. The network verifies the work through commitments, heartbeats, proofs, or other approved mechanisms.
4. The participant may earn SYN rewards from the approved rewards pool.
5. SYN may be used for staking, governance, rewards, access, or other SYNTHOS network functions.
6. More useful immune-node participation strengthens the network.

Rewards are paid **per verified operator**, not per node. A verified operator may run one node or one hundred nodes, but the operator receives only one standard reward stream unless governance approves a separate special-purpose program.

Operator rewards are paid **one month in arrears**. An operator must run the node through the reward period first, then claim payment for the month just completed. This keeps SYNTHOS from paying forward for uptime that has not happened yet.

## 9. Operator Payment Requirements

To receive immune-node operator rewards, an operator must satisfy the following requirements.

### 9.1 Verified Operator Status

Each operator must register as a verified SYNTHOS operator before earning rewards. Verification may include:

- a valid wallet address controlled by the operator;
- a unique hardware or device commitment;
- any required KYC, sanctions, fraud, or eligibility review;
- acceptance of SYNTHOS operator terms;
- agreement to follow network, security, and community rules; and
- confirmation that the operator is not attempting to bypass the one-operator reward limit.

Rewards are paid to the verified operator wallet. The operator is responsible for securing that wallet.

### 9.2 One Reward Stream Per Operator

Rewards are paid per verified operator, not per node, device, wallet, endpoint, server, household, or installation.

A verified operator may run multiple immune nodes, but the operator receives only one standard reward stream unless SYNTHOS governance creates a separate special-purpose program.

Operators may not split, duplicate, sell, rent, proxy, or misrepresent identities to claim multiple reward streams.

### 9.3 Required Node Operation

To earn a heartbeat reward for a reward period, the operator must:

- run an approved SYNTHOS immune node;
- keep the node online and reachable according to current network rules;
- submit valid heartbeat proofs during the reward period;
- maintain the required minimum uptime for the reward period;
- avoid duplicate, fake, replayed, or automated-farm heartbeat submissions;
- keep node software reasonably current with required security updates;
- avoid behavior that harms the network, other operators, users, or the SYNTHOS ecosystem; and
- comply with any additional technical requirements published before or during the reward period.

### 9.4 Heartbeat Proofs

Heartbeat proofs are the primary proof that an operator kept an immune node active during the reward period.

Each valid heartbeat proof should show that the operator's node was alive, participating, and bound to the registered operator identity or hardware commitment. SYNTHOS may reject heartbeat proofs that are incomplete, duplicated, suspicious, late, replayed, or inconsistent with network records.

### 9.5 Payment Timing

Operator heartbeat rewards are paid one month in arrears.

This means the operator must complete the reward period first. After the period ends, the operator may claim the heartbeat reward for the month just completed, subject to verification and available rewards.

No operator is entitled to payment in advance for uptime that has not happened yet.

### 9.6 Reward Amounts

The initial operator reward policy is:

| Reward Type | Amount | Timing | Requirement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Early verified operator reward | 500 SYN | One time | Paid once to an approved early operator after registration and eligibility verification |
| Operator heartbeat reward | 1,000 SYN | Monthly in arrears | Paid after a completed heartbeat period with valid proof and required uptime |

SYNTHOS governance may adjust future reward amounts, eligibility standards, claim windows, proof requirements, or reward caps, but changes should be disclosed clearly before they apply to future reward periods.

### 9.7 Forfeiture and Disqualification

An operator may lose eligibility for a reward period, have rewards delayed, or be disqualified from the program if the operator:

- submits false, duplicate, manipulated, or replayed heartbeat proofs;
- attempts to claim more than one standard reward stream;
- uses bots, farms, rented identities, fake devices, or other sybil behavior;
- attacks, degrades, exploits, or abuses the SYNTHOS network;
- violates operator terms, sanctions rules, compliance requirements, or applicable law;
- sells or transfers verified operator status without approval;
- refuses required verification; or
- otherwise acts in bad faith toward SYNTHOS, operators, users, or the network.

Disqualified rewards remain in the Immune Node Rewards allocation unless governance approves another use.

### 9.8 No Guaranteed Employment or Income

Immune-node operator rewards are network participation incentives. They are not wages, salary, employment compensation, or a guarantee of income.

Reward eligibility depends on proof of participation, compliance with program rules, available reward funding, and the continued operation of the SYNTHOS rewards program.
