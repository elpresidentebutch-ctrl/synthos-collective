## 2. Immediate Action You Can Do Today ($0 — Common Law `™`)

1. **Right to Use `™` Today (Nov 22, 2026):**
   * In the United States, **`™`** indicates a common-law trademark claim on **unregistered** marks. It costs **$0** and requires no government filing, so **SYNTHOS Collective™**, **www.IshamWilliamsBlockchains™**, **James Isham Williams Sr.™**, and **Immortal Anchor™** can carry **`™`** today (subject to verifying final USPTO conflict clearance below).
   * **Never use `®`** until the USPTO issues an official Certificate of Registration (premature `®` use is unlawful under 15 U.S.C. § 1111).
