# SYNTHOS Token Risk Disclosure

This disclosure is a public-facing draft for launch review. It is not legal,
financial, tax, or investment advice. Participants should review the final
published terms and consult their own advisors before interacting with SYNTHOS.

## No Price Promise

SYN is intended for participation in the SYNTHOS ecosystem. No statement in
project materials should be read as a promise of profit, price appreciation, or
guaranteed liquidity. Market value can rise, fall, or become unavailable.

## Smart Contract Risk

SYNTHOS contracts may contain bugs, integration errors, or unexpected behavior.
Audits and tests reduce risk but do not eliminate it. Users can lose funds if a
contract, wallet, bridge, DEX, RPC, frontend, or dependency fails.

## Treasury Recycling Burn

SYNTHOS includes a Treasury Recycling Burn mechanism for approved protocol
spend categories. When used, half of the spent SYN is removed from supply and
half is sent to the configured treasury. Normal ERC-20 transfers are not taxed
by this mechanism. Burning supply does not guarantee token value.

## Governance and Admin Risk

Launch administration uses a sovereign SYNTHOS multisig, timelock, and
governance path. These controls can manage protocol parameters and emergency
actions. Key loss, signer compromise, governance failure, or timelock misuse can
harm the ecosystem.

## Liquidity Risk

DEX pools may have shallow liquidity, volatile prices, slippage, or temporary
imbalance. Users should check quotes and pool reserves before swapping. Initial
pool configuration does not guarantee future liquidity.

## Eligibility and Jurisdiction Risk

Access to rewards, grants, distributions, or launch programs may depend on
jurisdiction, compliance records, disclosures, wallet verification, or other
eligibility criteria. Some participants may be restricted or removed from
programs.

## Validator and Operator Risk

Validator, node, and adopter reward programs may require hardware, uptime,
proofs, registration, eligibility, and continued compliance. Rewards can be
changed, paused, capped, delayed, or discontinued through the protocol control
process.

## Custody Risk

Users are responsible for securing their own wallets, seed phrases, private
keys, and devices. The project cannot recover lost private keys or reverse
valid on-chain transactions.

## Network and Infrastructure Risk

RPC endpoints, explorers, indexers, frontends, relays, and peer infrastructure
may fail, return stale data, or be attacked. Users should verify critical
transactions independently.

## Regulatory Risk

Rules for digital assets can change. Regulatory actions, legal requirements,
tax rules, or jurisdictional restrictions may affect the availability or
operation of SYNTHOS.

## Participant Acknowledgment

By interacting with SYNTHOS contracts or programs, participants acknowledge that
they understand these risks and are not relying on any guarantee of profit,
liquidity, uninterrupted access, or reward availability.
